Hi, I am **Eric Wang**.

I write about the engineering work behind modern technology systems: infrastructure, DevOps, SRE, Web3, trading systems, and AI. This blog is where I organize technical notes, explain complex systems in plain language, and document ideas that are useful for builders who care about reliability, automation, and practical architecture.

## What I Write About

- **Infrastructure and DevOps**: cloud platforms, automation, deployment workflows, observability, and operational tooling.
- **SRE and reliability**: system resilience, incident thinking, monitoring, alerting, and engineering practices that keep services stable.
- **Web3 and blockchain**: foundational concepts, protocol design, smart contracts, decentralized systems, and practical ecosystem notes.
- **Trading systems**: data pipelines, market monitoring, automation, risk controls, and system design for time-sensitive workflows.
- **AI engineering**: applied AI tools, agent workflows, model integration, and how AI changes software delivery.

## Writing Style

My goal is to make technical topics easier to understand without removing the engineering detail that matters. I prefer clear explanations, practical examples, and structured thinking over hype.

The posts here are written for engineers, operators, technical founders, and curious readers who want to understand how systems work beneath the surface.
